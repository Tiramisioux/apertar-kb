## Q. Questions a patch author still has to answer

Each: question · where to look · why it matters · status.

1. **Is the grace long enough for a single >1.5× jump?** CC:8036-8044 (`max(8, requests_+4)` frames of the slower rate), 8047-8053 (from `CLOCK_BOOTTIME` now), 8161-8167 (gap estimator returns inside grace), CC:7999-8000@25397cd (ratio test at 1.5×). K5: the first delta that shows the new period lands ≈ t0 + (N+3)·T_old; grace = (N+4)·T_old on a ramp-up → **1 frame of margin**, inside the ±1 uncertainty of K5. A miss = one false `+1` and the clip-wide drop latch (`RecordingDropWarning.hpp:10-28`). Only jumps > 1.5× are exposed (WebUI button 25→50, `ramp_fps seconds=0`, PCF list 30→50); 1-fps steps never trip it (r2 U3). **PROBABLE.** Settle on the Pi: print `delta_us` next to `frame_us`/`prev_us` on the `frame=` line (CC:8096-8097) and set `APERTAR_FPS_RAMP_GRACE_FRAMES` from the measured lag.
2. **Why a grace at all, rather than the delta-vs-previous-duration rule?** K3+K4: delta(n) = FrameDuration(n−1) by construction, so `gap iff delta > 1.5 × FrameDuration(n−1)` needs no window and stays armed through a ramp. With the grace, a 25→50 stepper (25 steps × 200 ms = 5-10 frames apart, App:9192-9199) keeps the estimator and the phase lock off for the whole 5 s + grace: Core now suppresses exactly what r1 §3 faulted in Cinemate (RL:604-630@d6cc93e). **CONFIRMED gap** (design not justified in the patch); the alternative rule is PROBABLE until K4 is measured.
3. **Pairing threshold 2 % misses 1-fps steps at ≥ 50 fps.** CC:7764 (`|Δ| > prev/50`). 25→26: 3.85 % (paired); 48→49: 2.04 %; 50→51: 1.96 %; 60→61: 1.6 %; 100→101: 0.99 % → no `frame=` line, `fps-ramp.txt` rows read `- -` (CC:8130-8136), and the next larger jump pairs the *oldest* event (CC:8094-8109). **CONFIRMED gap** (arithmetic). Fix: threshold relative to the requested step, or pair on `|frame_us − 1e6/toFps| < tolerance`.
4. **`record_start` while the stepper runs.** App:9174-9207 (thread keeps running; T31 §7 "by design"); CC:6193 (`recordingBaseFps_ = settings.fps` = the mid-ramp value); CC:7474-7480 (first frame accepted only when its `FrameDuration` is within `max(500 µs, 2 %)` of `1e6/settings_.fps`; a 1-fps step at 25 is 1539 µs and the sensor is always ≥ N+2 frames behind → never ready until the ramp ends). Result: a late start with base fps = e.g. 37 in every tag and in the WAV. **CONFIRMED gap.** Decide: refuse `record_start` while `fpsRampThreadActive_`, or `stopFpsRamp()` first.
5. **Settings persisted on every step; the mid-ramp fps survives a stop.** CC:9150-9151 (`persistSettingsLocked()` in every `applySettingsLocked`), App:9192-9199 → 25 settings-file rewrites in 5 s during a take; a take stopped mid-ramp leaves `fps=37` persisted and the next take starts there (Cinemate lesson b, `ff59dd8`: a take-scoped value must not become the operator's target; `dynamic_resolution_deferred_fps`, cinemate CC:1814-1816, :2378-2416@d6cc93e). **CONFIRMED** (persist per step) · decision (restore `recordingBaseFps_` at `record_stop`? skip persist while `rampingTake`?).
6. **Keep-exposure forgets the nominal after a clamp.** CC:8792-8806: `shutterUs` clamped to `floor(1e6/fps)`, nothing stored. 40000 µs @25 → 50 clamps to 20000 → back to 25 stays 20000 (180°, was 360°). Cinemate keeps `exposure_time_nominal` and recomputes per step (cinemate CC:1839-1842@d6cc93e). **CONFIRMED gap.**
7. **Clamp value vs realizable exposure.** CC:8801 clamps to the full frame; K7 says the sensor takes at most frame − 4 lines (libcamera) and VMAX − 8/16 lines (driver) ≈ 30-120 µs less at 7.4 µs/line. UI/setting overstate by that; DNG is right (K10). **CONFIRMED** (small; document).
8. **Auto shutter under a ramp.** CC:8797 (`!shutterAuto` → neither policy runs); IPA:1805-1809 (`setMaxExposureTime` on each limits change), agc_channel.cpp:389-398. With AGC on, a ramp-up shortens exposure and the AGC re-balances gain; `policy=` does nothing; T31 §5.3 tests manual only. **PROBABLE.** Document, or refuse ramps with `shutterAuto`.
9. **Gain compensation under `policy=angle`.** −1 stop per doubling (CC:8795); no ISO change. Cinemate none either (r1 §2). **Decision / UNKNOWN** whether wanted.
10. **Pot jitter = ramp storm.** App:8491-8497 (`analogPresetIndex` = `llround`, no hysteresis), 8565-8572 (dispatch on any preset change); GroveAnalogInput.cpp:306-315 (`deadband` config exists). A pot at a boundary toggles `set_fps` → each toggle re-arms the grace (CC:8052) and persists (Q5); with `fps_ramp` on and recording, the estimator and the lock never leave grace. **PROBABLE** (depends on the rig's deadband).
