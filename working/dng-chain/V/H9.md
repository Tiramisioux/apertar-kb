## 3. Lens 3 — mechanism and plausibility

- **Soak stall arithmetic** (`hb:963-967`): pool 173 frames x 16.9 MB = 2924 MB ≈ "2941 MB" (MiB/MB rounding). Pool full in 15-22 s at ~21 fps means the disk accepted ~21 − 173/(15..22) ≈ 9.5-13 fps ≈ 160-220 MB/s on ntfs-3g (FUSE) — plausible for FUSE NTFS on a Pi 5. It says nothing about exFAT (in-kernel) throughput. **plausible / confirmed as recorded**.
- **12:U = 2 B/px**: established in upstream libcamera (`pisp.cpp:1214-1220, 142-147`) and cinepi-raw (`rpicam_app.cpp:118`, `dng_output_depth.hpp:17-26`). Consequence for the 4K 50 fps SDR case on cinemate: CFE writes 16.59 MB/frame = **829 MB/s** into DRAM at 50 fps, the BE reads it, the encode worker reads it again and packs to 12.44 MB (622 MB/s to disk); Core's COMP1 path writes/reads 8.29 MB (415 MB/s) and decodes. cinemate's SDR path is heavier on DRAM than Core's, which cuts against the premise that cinemate would do better at 4K 50 fps. **confirmed (bytes), probable (consequence)**.
- **"Excel-derived" 38 fps on exFAT NVMe** (May 2026): 38 x 12.44 MB = **473 MB/s** sustained to an exFAT NVMe through cinepi-raw's 4-encode/2-disk-worker profile, uncounted, duration null. Plausible on a Gen 3 x1 NVMe (kb dd-direct 733 MB/s) for a take shorter than one volume pass; not a 50 fps record and not ClearHDR.
- No kernel or hardware claim in H9 is load-bearing beyond these; WebSearch/WebFetch were not needed (all sources are on disk).

## 5. Better test

E6/X1 (S3, q7) is right but can be made cheaper and more decisive:

1. **Offline first, no Pi session:** the operator's cinemate clips are already a counted record. cinepi-raw numbers frames continuously and skips indices on a drop (`docs/playback.md:80`: "DROP N — a gap in cinepi-raw's own frame-numbering, read from the filenames on disk"; `docs/cli-commands.md:164`). For every `CINEPI_*_cam0` folder on his media: `ls *.dng | wc -l`, min/max frame index from the filenames, gaps = (max − min + 1) − count; read mode, bit depth, fps and `--log-encode` state from one DNG's tags (`exiftool`: ImageWidth/Height, BitsPerSample, LinearizationTable present → Log; FrameRate tag if written) and the file size (16.59 MB = linear 16-bit, 12.44 MB = Log 12 or 12-bit SDR). This gives counted drop numbers per clip for whatever he actually shot, at zero cost.
2. **On the rig, same drive:** boot the cinemate card; record `findmnt /media/RAW` (fstype, options), `ps -o args= -C cinepi-raw` (`--mode`, `--max-pixel-rate`, `--buffer-count`), `md5sum` of the installed `imx585.json` vs `resources/tuning_files/imx585.json`, `sudo fstrim -v /media/RAW` output (volume trim state matters after the 0.9 TB finding); then 3 x 60 s per case: 4K 16-bit Clear HDR 25 fps **Log off**, the same with Log 12, 4K SDR 12-bit 50 fps (891 MHz, overclock on, dynamic resolution off). Read `droppedFrames`, `writeFailures`, `bufferSizeMax` (`redis-cli subscribe cp_stats`), `journalctl -u cinemate-autostart | grep -c 'Frame Drop'`, and DNG count vs 60 x fps.
