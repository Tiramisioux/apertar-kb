## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Plausible, not refuted; most of it is confirmed arithmetic.** The link rate (1782 Mbps/lane), the D-PHY clamp, the HMAX 550 / 60 fps ceiling, the
CSI-2 utilisation and the drive ratios all re-compute as stated. Three parts need rewording: (1) "identical on both rigs" is confirmed for the link
rate on both config.txt files and probable for the RP1 clock, but the two stacks apply **different software pixel-rate caps** (646.7 vs 580, or 380
under cinemate's mtime guard), and the cinemate rig's config was last read on 2026-08-31; (2) "~80 % of the RP1 pixel rate" is the sensor's
**line rate** against the **formula** cap and is fps-independent — 89 % against cinemate's 580, and no rig has measured the hardware ceiling;
(3) "above anything sustained so far (525)" is true but 525 MB/s was delivery-limited, so it is a floor on the drive, not a ceiling. The
"marginal for the drive even if capture is fixed" statement is fair as an inference (85 % of a once-measured O_DIRECT ceiling, with exFAT per-file
costs never measured at that rate) and has **no counted take behind it**. **No USB SSD figure exists in the kb or the handbook**; the USB claim is
a domain inference from the 5 Gbps port and published Pi 5 figures (300–420 MB/s), probable but unmeasured on this rig.

## 2. Arithmetic re-done (3840×2160; 4 lanes; MB = 10^6 B)

### 2.1 CSI-2 payload and utilisation

| Bits | Payload / frame | 50 fps | 60 fps | 50 fps vs 4×1440 / 4×1500 / 4×1782 | 60 fps vs 4×1440 / 4×1500 / 4×1782 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | 12,441,600 B | 622.1 MB/s = 4.977 Gbit/s | 746.5 MB/s = 5.972 Gbit/s | **86.4 % / 82.9 % / 69.8 %** | **103.7 % (impossible) / 99.5 % / 83.8 %** |
| 10 | 10,368,000 B | 518.4 MB/s = 4.147 Gbit/s | 622.1 MB/s = 4.977 Gbit/s | 72.0 / 69.1 / 58.2 % | 86.4 / 82.9 / 69.8 % |
| 16 (3840×2160 part of the 2200-row Clear HDR frame), 25 fps | 16,588,800 B | 414.7 MB/s = 3.318 Gbit/s | — | 57.6 / 55.3 / **46.5 %** | — |

Packet overhead ≈ 6 B per line packet + 2 short packets per frame ≈ 13 kB/frame (0.1 %): negligible (R9 §6 same).

**Per-line occupancy** (the fps-independent number, since the sensor sends one 12-bit row per HMAX): 12-bit row = (4 + 5760 + 2) × 8 bits / 4 lanes =
11,532 bit/lane → 6.47 µs at 1782, 7.69 µs at 1500, 8.01 µs at 1440, against the 7.407 µs line at HMAX 550 → **87 % at 1782; a 12-bit row does not
fit at ≤ 1500 Mbps**, which is why the driver uses HMAX 660 (8.889 µs, 90 %) at 720 MHz. The 69.8 % frame-average at 50 fps = 87 % × (2160 active
of 2700 VMAX lines). H12's "~70 % of the link" is therefore right as a frame average and understates the per-line load; the honest triple is
**70 % average / 87 % per line / 83 % of the D-PHY's calibrated 1500 Mbps bin**.

## 4. Mechanism and domain

| Claim | Mechanism / plausibility | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-PHY at 1782 on the 1500 code "costs receiver margin, not throughput" | The hsfreqrange code is the Synopsys D-PHY RX's coarse clock/data-recovery calibration band; out of band the receiver still locks (both rigs stream for minutes) but with less margin; the failure mode seen at 2079 was row truncation over minutes, not refusal. The PHY setting is mode-invariant (one `link_freq` control set at probe), so it cannot separate 12-bit from 16-bit cases, and it is identical on both rigs. | probable (handbook measurements + kernel code); CRC/ECC error rate at 50 fps **unconfirmed** (`csi2_errors` debugfs never read at 50 fps) |
| The RP1 cap is a minimum pixel-processing time, so what matters is the line rate 518.4 MPix/s, not the frame average | `minPixelProcessingTime = 1.0us / rate`; the IPA stretches `minLineLength` to `width × that`; confirmed in the handbook by the 43.80/66.85 ratio = 380/580 (`:337-399`) and by the kb's reading of the qbc IPA (`q6-link.md:182-217`) | confirmed (behaviour) |
| 622 MB/s on PCIe Gen 3 ×1 behind a CFexpress hat is "marginal" | Raw 985 MB/s; the one O_DIRECT measurement is 733 (74 % of raw, normal for ×1 NVMe with TLP overhead and a DRAM-less controller without HMB); exFAT buffered writes at 50 files/s add per-file `sync_file_range`, 1 s per-file fsync (= device FLUSH, H11), FAT/bitmap/directory updates and `lru_add_drain_all` per DONTNEED (H3) — none measured at > 525 MB/s. Published Pi 5 Gen 3 ×1 sustained writes 550–720 (R6 d.1) bracket 622 at the top. The pSLC cache (≤ 64 GB) is gone within ~2 min at 622 MB/s; the E27T's ≈ 800 MB/s post-cache rate is above the link, so the link + filesystem are the wall. ### 5.2 Is "4K 50 fps 12-bit uncompressed is marginal for the drive even if capture is fixed" fair?

**Yes, as a labelled inference.** It rests on arithmetic against a once-measured O_DIRECT ceiling, on published Pi 5 Gen 3 ×1 envelopes that

   (post-pSLC curve; expect the knee and the steady rate), then the file-pattern run
   `fio --name=dng --rw=write --bs=12M --filesize=12M --nrfiles=3000 --file_service_type=sequential --fsync=1 --fadvise_hint=1 --directory=/media/RAW/fiot`

5. **USB (if the operator's USB SSDs matter):** `lsusb -t` (uas vs usb-storage), then step 2's two fio runs on the USB volume (100 GB is enough
