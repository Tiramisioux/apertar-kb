# V/H4 — WebUI MJPEG downsample on libcamera's completion thread as the first candidate for the 10-31 short-take capture gaps

Verdict: **refuted as formulated** (confidence 0.6). The code facts hold (thread, rate, bytes, encode off-thread). Two load-bearing parts do not: the cost was profiled (kb 10-25, about 0.2-0.3 ms per frame, not "0.2-3 ms, never profiled"), and the 10-31 gap pattern needs a steady per-frame term of about 1.3-4.4 ms that varies 10x between takes with the WebUI state constant. The downsample survives as a small contributor and a cheap hygiene fix, not as the lead cause.

### 1.4 Bytes moved per frame (confirmed arithmetic)

`scaledWebPreviewSize(1280, 720)` → scale 0.5 → 640x360 (APP:4330-4344). `downsampleNearestPlane` takes the `halfWidth` NEON path (`vld2q_u8` reads 32 source bytes per 16 output bytes, i.e. the whole source row) for every output row; only every other source row is touched (APP:4346-4398, NEON at :4378-4383).

| Plane | Rows read | Bytes per row | Read | Written |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Y | 360 of 720 | 1280 | 460,800 | 230,400 |
| U | 180 of 360 | 640 | 115,200 | 57,600 |
| V | 180 of 360 | 640 | 115,200 | 57,600 |
| total | | | **691,200 (0.69 MB)** | 345,600 |

M.md's "~0.7 MB" is right. R7 §5.1 (R7.md:131-132) says "≈ 576 KB" because it counted 57.6 KB per chroma plane: the chroma rows are read whole (640 B), so 115.2 KB each.

Writes go to a cached `std::vector` that is recycled between the capture and the encoder (`pendingMjpegFrame_` / `recycledMjpegYuv_`, APP:9405-9411, :9478-9479); at 25 fps with a few-ms encode no per-frame allocation occurs after the first frame (the vector alternates between the two owners).

### 1.6 cinepi-raw comparison (confirmed)

`RPiCamApp::requestComplete` → `post_processor_.Process(payload)` (cinepi-raw core/rpicam_app.cpp:1212, :1255); `PostProcessor::Process` spawns `std::thread{process_fn, ...}.detach()` per request (core/post_processor.cpp:107-136); the MJPEG stage does `BufferReadSync` + libjpeg compress of the 1280x720 lores on that thread, every frame, regardless of clients (cinepi/mjpegPreviewStage.cpp:290-320). Off the serial cycle, as H4 says. Caveat the hypothesis omits: the detached stage holds the request's buffers until `outputThread` requeues them, so slow stages cost buffers rather than cycle time; the handbook measured the Clear HDR preview stage at 21.9-35.2 ms per frame and recorded drops until `ea53b2c` cut it (cinemate-handbook/lessons/hardware-log.md:2226-2250, :3184-3203). "Off the serial cycle" is not "free of drops" on either stack.

### 2.3 Does 0.3-0.5 ms explain the 10-31 pattern?

Model: steady deficit d per frame; first skip after slack/d frames; then one skip every 40 ms / d frames (the kb's model, research-30fps-answer.md:16-19; HL:171-175).

| Take (HL:784-786) | Frames written | Gaps | First gap | d from first-gap timing (slack 130-220 ms over 50-100 frames) | d from the later gap spacing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 351 (~14 s) | 2 | 2-4 s in | 1.3-4.4 ms | 1 gap in 250-300 frames → not steady; a transient |
| 2 | 263 (~10.5 s) | 9 | 2-4 s in | 1.3-4.4 ms | 8 in 160-210 frames → every 20-26 frames → **1.5-2 ms** |
| 3 | 145 (~5.8 s) | 4 | 2-4 s in | 1.3-4.4 ms | 3 in 45-95 frames → every 15-32 → 1.3-2.7 ms |
| 4 (Log on) | 117 (~4.7 s) | 10 | 2-4 s in | 1.3-4.4 ms | 9 in 17-67 frames → every 2-7 frames → **6-20 ms** |

Observations: the WebUI state was the same in all four; the implied deficit spans 0 (transient) to >6 ms; three takes need a steady 1.3-2.7 ms, one needs 6-20 ms. A constant 0.3-0.5 ms term cannot produce any of these on its own, and cannot produce the 10x spread. It can be 10-30 % of a ~2 ms deficit. The Log take is the worst and Log adds a 16→12 LUT pass on the copy worker (R7.md:187), i.e. extra DRAM traffic, which the kb has shown slows the BE/libcamera passes (HL:167); the hardware log's "Log cannot be the cause" (HL:792) is right for the other three takes, not as an exoneration for take 4.

## 4. The proposed test

The kb's two-take test (README:125; S3-1 E1) can show whether an open browser matters, but not why: a browser adds the downsample, the encoder thread, three HTTP threads per second and the page's `/api/state` under `commandMutex_`. With a ~0.3 ms term and a ~4 ms margin the honest expectation for "open" is 0 gaps as often as not, so a null result would be read as "WebUI innocent" when the real variable (post-reboot activity, first take, writeback state) was uncontrolled.

Better test (adds three things: isolation of the downsample, a direct cost measurement, and control of the confounders):

1. Conditions fixed: Clear HDR 16-bit 4K 25 fps uncompressed, Log off, pre-roll off, speed ramp off, drive trimmed, Pi idle ≥ 5 min after boot, no ssh commands during takes, the first take after any reconfigure discarded (CHD:119). Record `ss -tnp state established '( sport = :8080 )'` before each take.
2. Three clients, 60 s each, interleaved three times (9 takes): (a) no client; (b) `curl -sN http://<pi>:8080/preview.mjpeg > /dev/null &` as the only client (downsample + encoder, no page polling); (c) the WebUI page open and left alone.
3. During one (b) and one (c): `python3 -I usample.py <pid> <CameraManager tid> 20` → share of `downsampleNearestPlane`; and one `LIBCAMERA_LOG_LEVELS=RPI:0` window for `be_cycle.py` S4 medians.
4. Read `dropped_gap_estimate`, frames written vs 1500, `peak_queue`, `dropped_copy_slot`.
5. Decision: H4 holds only if (b) shows gaps in ≥ 2 of 3 runs while (a) shows none, and S4 rises by ≥ 1 ms with the client. If (c) shows gaps and (b) does not, the page's polling or thread churn is the term, not the downsample. If (a) also shows gaps, the cause is the environment (post-reboot/first-take/writeback) and H4 is out.

Fix notes (improvement, independent of the verdict): capping the web preview at 10-12 fps while recording divides the term by 2-3 with no structural change (APP:80 as a recording-time value). Moving the three passes to `mjpegThread_` removes it from the cycle but needs the preview buffer to stay valid while the worker reads it: the completion thread requeues the request right after (`requeueRequest`, CC:8673), so a worker read races the BE's next write into that slot (tearing on a preview, acceptable) unless the request is held (costs one of 4 preview buffers). A 0.69 MB copy on the completion thread costs about what the downsample costs, so "copy then hand off" gains nothing.
