## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

Confirmed, with corrections. (a) Every long clean take of 4K 25 fps Clear HDR 16-bit is on a build up to `a40112a`; on `d44b177` and later the hardware log records **no** Clear HDR 16-bit 25 fps take at all (not "none longer than ~14 s": zero). The four short takes (351/263/145/117 frames, 2-10 capture gaps, WebUI open) and the clean ~3-min lossless take (4476 frames) are on the **pre-squash** drop-frames builds of 2026-10-31 22:26/22:54, which contain trim + Log + the ram-queue code but not the pre-roll, the Clear HDR override or the safety stop (d44b177 message: "Run on the Pi so far (an earlier build) ... Not compiled or run yet: the pre-roll and the Clear HDR override. Not run yet: the ram queue, the safety stop ..."). (b) No 4K 50 fps take exists after T34 M3 (10-28, `4d5fdd1`); the only faster 4K take since is the 2 s, 60 fps, lossless pre-roll on d44b177, whose drop counters are disabled by design. (c) d44b177 adds three per-frame operations on libcamera's completion thread (two ControlList lookups and a handful of atomics for the pre-roll), microseconds against a ~36 ms serial cycle; it adds one full-frame table-lookup pass on the copy worker when Log is on in 16-bit (extra DRAM traffic, the one mechanism the kb has measured to cost capture frames); the ram queue changes only the queue-full decision and is off by default; trim and pre-roll never run inside a take. So "nothing shortens the serial cycle" holds; "nothing runs on libcamera's thread" should read "nothing that costs measurable time". (d) The frame-numbering claim is confirmed in code at all three relevant builds (`4d5fdd1`, `a40112a`, `92d0970`): numbering is time-based with the speed ramp off (a capture gap leaves a hole) and count-based with it on (no hole, ever). T34 M3's `missing=355 = dropped_gap_estimate=355` is the ramp-off behaviour and the log says ramp off: consistent. A2's `missing=0` with 1491 gaps has exactly one code path: the speed ramp was **on** during A2 (probable). Hence README finding 7 ("takes.sh missing cannot see capture gaps", labelled "measured") is wrong as a general rule, and the A0-A2 matrix carries an unrecorded ramp-on confounder. (e) The first half of H16 ("yes for the queue-full class") is established by inference, not by an end-to-end test: fstrim cures the stall (71 synthetic runs, robust); Core runs fstrim at mount (measured once, 927 GB in 0.47 s); Core's trim-after-delete has never been exercised across a drive pass (README open item 4) and touch-UI deletes bypass it (S3-11).

## 2. Check 1: "no take longer than ~14 s of 4K 25 fps Clear HDR 16-bit on d44b177 or later"

Observation (table rows 50-55): on `d44b177` and later, the log records three recording events, none of them Clear HDR 16-bit: the 60 fps lossless pre-roll (row 53), the SDR Log test (row 54), the ram-buffer safety-stop take (row 55). The Clear HDR 16-bit takes of the drop-frames branch (rows 50-51) are on the pre-squash builds of 10-31; the longest is 351 frames = 14.0 s at 25 fps (the source of H16's "~14 s").

Conclusion: the claim is true but misattributed. Correct wording: "on d44b177 and later there is no Clear HDR 16-bit 25 fps take at all; on the pre-squash drop-frames builds of 10-31 the longest is 14 s, and all four lost 2-10 frames at capture with the WebUI open". Also missing from H16: the ~3-min lossless take with no drop line on the same build (row 52). It does not bear on the Clear HDR 16-bit case (CHDR16 lossless at 24-25 fps is encoder-bound and fills the 24-frame queue in ~6 s, rows 37-39, so a clean 3-min lossless take is almost certainly SDR 12-bit), but it is the only long take on any drop-frames build and it was clean. Status: confirmed (hl:779-795, 813-877; d44b177 commit message).

## 3. Check 2: "no 4K 50 fps take after T34 M3"

Observation: after rows 35-36 (10-28, `4d5fdd1`, 3840x2076, untrimmed drive at 78-82 %, ramp off) the only 4K take above 30 fps is the pre-roll (row 53): 2.0 s at 60 fps, lossless, 4K SDR 12-bit, 115 frames, counters disabled by design (README:62; CC d44b177 diff `planQueueBuffer` → `publishStop` returns with `stopPercent_ = 0` for `prerollTake_`; the pre-roll is "left out of ... counters, the safety stop, the dropped-frames warning"). 115 frames in a take reported as 2.0 s is 57.5 fps average, consistent with a first-to-last-timestamp fps of 60.0 over ~1.9 s; it says nothing about drops either way. The ladder tests of 10-28 (hl:604) requested 50 fps and were capped to 25/30. 1080p at 50 fps is clean (row 32) but is not 4K.

Conclusion: confirmed. No 4K 50 fps take exists after T34 M3; none exists at 3840x2160 (the probe-authoritative ceiling of 60 arrived on 10-31, hl:827), on a trimmed drive, or on any build after `4d5fdd1`. The second operator case has exactly one measurement pair (rows 35-36), both on an untrimmed drive at 78-82 % full, with the WebUI state not recorded.

### 5.1 Code (identical at `4d5fdd1`, `a40112a`, `92d0970`)

{
