# V/H10 — The storage pre-roll: no stated mechanism, no measured effect, small code-grounded side effects

Verdict: **confirmed, with corrections** (confidence 0.85). The negative core of H10 (no author-stated mechanism in either
stack, no measurement of the next take's drops in either stack) holds under an exhaustive grep of cinemate, its docs, its
commits, the handbook and the kb. The three side effects named in H10 exist in Apertar's code at 92d0970 but two of them are
conditional in ways H10 does not say (the 1 GiB trim threshold depends on the mode and the seconds; the gap-counter grace
does not occur at all in the operator's Clear HDR 16-bit case and lasts 0.24 s in the 50 fps case). One of H10's two
"possible benefits" ("exFAT metadata cached") is refuted as a mechanism: the exFAT allocation bitmap is pinned in memory from
mount, and a contiguous DNG never touches the FAT. The other ("raw copy pool warm") is real but is a first-take effect of
~4-6 allocations in the take's first quarter second, which no recorded drop pattern matches.

Commits: Apertar-Core 92d0970 (`/home/user/Apertar-Core`), Apertar-UI e061be8, cinemate 4990afc, handbook and kb as on disk;
kernel rpi-6.12.y via raw.githubusercontent.com (WebFetch); labels: confirmed / probable / unconfirmed as in BRIEF.md.

### 2.2 Side effect h: the 1 GiB threshold, per mode (default 2 s)

`result.bytes` = the sum of the clip's file sizes (CC:11538-11545) compared with 1 GiB = 1 073.7 MB (App:10869).

| Mode | Pre-roll fps | Bytes per frame | 2 s | >= 1 GiB at 2 s? | Seconds to cross | Label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4K Clear HDR 16-bit uncompressed (case 1) | 25 (back-end cap = the operator's fps) | 16.9 MB (3840x2200x2; kb test files 16.9 MB hl:705) | 845 MB | no | 3 (1.27 GB) | probable (sizes from kb) |
| same, Log on | 25 | 12.45 MB (hl:819-821) | 623 MB | no | 4 | probable |
| 4K 12-bit SDR lossless (the measured run) | 60 | 5.98 MB (688/115) | 688 MB | no | 4 (3 s = 1.03 GB < 1.0737) | confirmed (hl:842-855) |
| 4K 12-bit SDR uncompressed (case 2 if uncompressed) | 60 | ~12.4 MB (745 MB/s / 60, hl:838) | ~1.49 GB if written | **yes, if the drive takes >= 537 MB/s for 2 s**; the 60 fps burst exceeds what the drive sustained (hl:838-839), so part of it is dropped queue-full inside the pre-roll (counted, muted) and the written bytes are what the drive took plus the 12-frame drain | probable; settle by reading `storage_preroll_last` in that mode |

So H10's "below the 1 GiB threshold" is mode-dependent. In the operator's case 2 with uncompressed cDNG the default pre-roll
likely crosses it (and reports its own drops every Core start, since it runs at 60 fps although he records at 50).

### 2.4 Benefit f: the raw copy pool

| Fact | Ref |
| --- | --- |
| `acquireRawBuffer(size)` takes `mutex_` (the queue's), and when no pooled vector fits returns `std::vector<uint8_t>(size)` **inside the lock**: a 12.4-16.9 MB zero-filled allocation (mmap + page faults + memset) | CC:4623-4645 |
| libcamera's completion thread takes the same `mutex_` in `hasCapacityForFrame` every frame | CC:4570-4572, :8950 |
| The pool survives a take ("the next take's first frames would otherwise allocate and page in 16.6 MB each on libcamera's completion thread"); at most `preferredRawPoolSize()` = min(maxQueuedFrames_, max(workerCount_ + 2, 4)) buffers are kept (4-6 with 12 queued frames and 2-4 writers) | CC:4384-4387, :5132-5153, :5232-5235 |
| Every camera configure clears it (`recorder_.clearRawPool()`), so a resolution / bit-depth / Clear HDR change after the automatic start-up pre-roll discards the warmth; the pre-roll itself never reconfigures | CC:8191; CC:11420-11421 |

Estimate (unconfirmed, arithmetic): 4-6 allocations x (4 100 page faults + two 16.9 MB zero passes) ~ 5-10 ms each, in the
first 4-6 frames of the first take after Core start or a mode change, each hold shorter than one frame period at 25 fps
(40 ms) and about a half period at 50 fps (20 ms). This is H5's territory; a pre-roll removes it for the first take only,
exactly as any earlier take in the same mode does. No recorded drop pattern sits in the first 0.25 s of a take (§3).

| Fact | Ref |
| --- | --- |
| `applyControls` calls `noteFpsChange` only when `settings_.fps != sanitized.fps` | CC:7175-7178 |
| Grace = max(10, N + 6) frames of the slower rate, N = `requests_.size()` ("4 on ext4, 6 on exFAT/NTFS, 8-10 with Clear HDR", comment); `APERTAR_FPS_RAMP_GRACE_FRAMES` overrides | CC:9209-9229 (comment :9214-9218), :9235-9253 |

in a mode whose pre-roll fps differs from the user's fps. It hides at most 12-16 frames of gap *detection*; it drops nothing.

Is the kb's proposed test (README:229-257) able to settle it? Only partly:
1. The COLD arm begins with a remount; on the drop-frames build a remount is an Insert trigger and runs a pre-roll 3 s after

   written, every COLD arm is a WARM arm.**
2. The SECOND arm is the right control for the pool/first-take effect, but every COLD take after the first one of a Core run

3. The WebUI state is not fixed; the 10-31 data show 2-10 gaps per short take with it open and 0 in 10-minute takes without,

4. It tests only 4K 25 fps Clear HDR 16-bit; in that mode the pre-roll changes no control at all (§2.5), so the arms differ

### 4.4 exFAT allocation pointer: where the next take lands after a deleted pre-roll (unnamed)

| Fact | Ref |
| --- | --- |
| `sbi->clu_srch_ptr = EXFAT_FIRST_CLUSTER` at mount (`exfat_read_boot_sector`) | fs/exfat/super.c |
| A new file's first cluster: `hint_clu = exfat_find_free_bitmap(sb, sbi->clu_srch_ptr)`; an extension tries `last_clu + 1` (`new_clu.dir = (last_clu == EXFAT_EOF_CLUSTER) ? EXFAT_EOF_CLUSTER : last_clu + 1`); on success `sbi->clu_srch_ptr = hint_clu`; a non-adjacent cluster converts the run to a FAT chain (`exfat_chain_cont_cluster`) | fs/exfat/fatent.c, inode.c |
| `exfat_free_cluster` / `__exfat_free_cluster` never touch `clu_srch_ptr` (only `exfat_alloc_cluster` does) | fs/exfat/fatent.c |

Consequence (probable, derived): after the pre-roll's delete the pointer sits at the pre-roll's last cluster, now free; the
next take's first DNG starts there and runs upward into untouched space, leaving a hole of (pre-roll clusters - 1): ~5 250
clusters for 688 MB, ~6 450 for 845 MB at 128 KB. The hole stays until a remount resets the pointer (the next boot's automatic
pre-roll of about the same size refills it) or the bitmap search wraps near a full volume. Holes do not accumulate: at most
one per pre-roll size exists at a time. When a later file straddles the hole's end, that one DNG (<= 129 clusters) gets a FAT
chain conversion of <= 129 entries into cached FAT buffers: once, sub-millisecond. Not a drop mechanism for per-frame DNG
(it would matter for one large MLV file). The untrimmed deleted LBAs are <= 1.5 GB per pre-roll against the ~0.9 TB stall
threshold (hl:701-735): 0.1 %, immaterial; modes >= 1 GiB trim at once (§2.2). R5's P9 worry is quantitatively void.

## 5. Corrections to H10's wording

1. "says only 'warmed up' / 'priming'": the `.py` says "warmed up" (storage_preroll.py:3-5); "priming"/"primed" are in
   `docs/settings-json.md:112` and `docs/cli-commands.md:179`; `docs/sensors.md:193` says "stress-tested" — the only
   purpose-like statement, and it describes a check, not a warming mechanism.
2. "ran once at 60.0 fps": the only *recorded* run; the same entry records a failed attempt (30 requested, ~24 actual,
   hl:846-849). With `[media] preroll` defaulting to on, every Core start and every card insert since the d44b177 build also
   runs one, unrecorded; whether the 10-31 short-take build had it is unconfirmed (hl:779-793, R7 §7).
3. "exFAT metadata cached" as a possible benefit: drop it. The bitmap is pinned from mount (balloc.c); contiguous files never
   touch the FAT (fatent.c).
4. "the deleted 0.7 GB is below the 1 GiB delete-trim threshold": true for 12-bit lossless (688 MB) and for Clear HDR 16-bit
   at 2 s (845 MB; 3 s crosses); in 4K 12-bit SDR uncompressed the default 2 s at 60 fps is ~1.49 GB if the drive takes it,
   so the delete-trim is probably queued every Core start in that mode. Say "mode-dependent" and give the table (§2.2).
5. "record_start kills a trim running > 2 s": add that a mount-time trim of this drive took 467 ms (hl:817-818) and that the
   pre-roll's own 5 s block delays the delete-trim by ~2 s, so the kill path needs a drive in the stalled state.
6. "the gap counter is blind for max(10, N+6) frames after each fps change": in the operator's Clear HDR 16-bit 25 fps case
   the pre-roll runs at 25 fps (the back-end cap, CC:3332-3342) and changes no control, so there is no grace; in the 50 fps
   case the grace after the restore is 12 frames = 0.24 s (N = 6 on exFAT), reached only by a take started inside it. It
   hides gap detection; it does not create drops. Refs: CC:9221-9259 (not "~9221-9244"), CC:9480-9486, CC:7175-7178.
7. Add the unnamed side effects: the automatic Insert trigger on every remount (invalidates the written COLD arm), record
   refused for ~3-5 s at Core start, the pre-roll's own "(N dropped)" in the uncompressed 12-bit mode.
8. The one code-grounded benefit (pool warm) is identical to any earlier take in the same mode and sits in the first 0.25 s;
   no recorded drop pattern (steady loss, minutes in, or 2-4 s after start) is where it acts.

## 6. Better test

Settle "does a pre-roll change the next take's drops" and separate it from "does *any* prior take" in one series:

- Environment: trimmed drive (`fstrim` once before the series), drive <= 55 C at each start, **WebUI closed on every device**,
  touch UI as the operator uses it, `APERTAR_STORAGE_PREROLL=0` in the unit's environment for the whole series (otherwise
  every Core restart runs a pre-roll by itself), `APERTAR_FPS_RAMP_GRACE_FRAMES` untouched, defaults otherwise.
- Core restarted before **every** take (`systemctl restart apertar-core`, wait for Ready + 10 s), so the raw pool, the
  governor state and the clip counter are identical at each start.
- Arms, interleaved C, P, S, C, P, S ... six of each per mode:
  - C (cold): record 120 s.
  - P (pre-roll): `storage_preroll` (manual), wait for `storage_preroll_active=false` + 5 s, record 120 s.
  - S (plain prior take): a 2 s take at the user's fps, kept, 5 s, record 120 s.
- Modes: 4K Clear HDR 16-bit 25 fps uncompressed (and with Log); 4K 12-bit SDR 50 fps in the operator's compression.
- Per take, from the journal and state: `drop[capture-gap]` lines with their time since start (bucket 0-1 s, 1-10 s,
  10-30 s, rest), `dropped_gap_estimate`, `dropped_queue_full`, `dropped_copy_slot`, `peak_queue`, frames written against
  fps x seconds; `storage_preroll_last` (bytes, "(N dropped)"), `media_trim_last_result/ms/unix` (did a delete-trim run, and
  when); `scaling_cur_freq` sampled at 10 Hz for the first 5 s; NVMe temperature.
- Decision: P differs from C beyond C's own spread in >= 5 of 6 pairs **and** beyond S's difference from C: the pre-roll has
  an effect of its own (then ask which: bytes, delete, trim). P ~ S != C: a first-take effect (pool, clock); fix it directly
  (allocate the pool at configure, governor) instead of a pre-roll. P ~ S ~ C: no effect; default `preroll=off` and keep the
  manual command as a drive check.
- One-time readbacks on the CM5 rig, before the series: `cat /proc/cmdline`,
  `cat /sys/module/nvme_core/parameters/default_ps_max_latency_us`,
  `cat /sys/devices/system/cpu/cpufreq/policy0/scaling_governor`,
  `cat /sys/devices/system/cpu/cpufreq/ondemand/{sampling_rate,up_threshold,sampling_down_factor,io_is_busy}`,
  `nvme id-ctrl /dev/nvme0 | grep -E "^fr|hmpre|hmmin"`.
