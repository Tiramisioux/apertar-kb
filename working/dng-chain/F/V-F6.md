## 4. What the report missed that changes the verdict

1. **Storage capacity precondition for (A)** (E4): no 10 min (A) test is possible above ≈ 55 % fill; the pSLC discussion is moot at this take length.
2. **Writer-side mechanisms are first-tier evidence, not second-tier** (E1/E2): the two cleanest capture-side experiments in the kb (T22 dd, A2) both vary the writeback path with everything else fixed. The roadmap's Path 1 should carry `APERTAR_DNG_WRITEBACK=1` and the O_DIRECT patch (D7) as an arm interleaved with T1, since T1 and D7 attack different suspects (BE bytes vs writeback/DMA disturbance) and M3 vs pre-roll could be explained by the latter (uncompressed 12 MB/frame page-cache traffic vs lossless 6 MB) as much as by the former.
3. **Which libcamera runs** decides (B)'s margin by ≈ 10 ms (swap+stats on or off the thread) and is also the first thing to read for the T1 A/B; F6 lists it as a question but does not make it a step-1.0 precondition for the (B) margin claim.
4. **IPA alternate-frame skipping is already on at 50 fps** (E8): the S1 row for (A) should be ≈ 0.8 ms average, not 1.6; it slightly improves (A)'s today-margin arithmetic but does not reach the 2.8 ms deficit.
5. Levers the kb/Core offer that F6 covers adequately: preview size/omission (C8, correct: BE still runs into internal buffers, `rpi_stream.cpp:111-117,136`), request depth (= buffer count, `CameraController.cpp:8421-8425`, correct), sensor mode (S1/S2), fps pinning (Core pins `FrameDurationLimits` only for phase lock and pre-roll, `:8637-8647`; nothing further to gain), DPC/GEQ/ALSC/sharpen/CAC (T3: compute-only in a pipelined hardware BE, plausible, unmeasured). **No missed lever of size**; the misses are preconditions and priorities.
6. Not checked and still open: how Core delivers the 1280x720 preview to HDMI (no DRM calls in Core's src; presumably the UI process) — if a copy ran on the completion thread it would belong in S4; the 2026-10-25 usample (0.23-0.5 ms for all of Core's completion-thread work, V/H4) bounds it anyway.
