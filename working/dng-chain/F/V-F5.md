## 3. Errors found

1. **Arithmetic, §2.1 row "data sustained in M3":** 11.4 MB/frame and ~500 MB/s come from dividing the log's rounded "30 GB" by 2632. The uncompressed 3840x2076 12-bit frame is 11.96 MB; the sustained rate was **~525 MB/s** (2632 × 11.96 MB / 60 s), the figure FEAS-BRIEF already fixed. Consequence: the (A) drive-margin row reads "622 is 18 % above the sustained 525", not 24 % above 500. Verdict unchanged.
2. **Overcount, §1 and §7:** "3 min clean × 5" and "5 of 5 three-minute takes clean on the default sync policy". Four counted (B) 3-min clean takes exist, on three different sync policies; two of them on today's default. The fifth is a lossless take of unstated (probably SDR) mode. Verdict unchanged; the sentence in §7 should read "4 of 4 (B) three-minute takes without the whole-filesystem syncfs were clean (2 of 2 on today's default policy)".
3. **Misclassification, §3.1 B21:** C0016 is SDR 12-bit lossless (measurements.md:85), not an unknown-mode (B) take. Its 310 missing in 30 holes on a 78-82 % never-trimmed drive belongs in the (A)-family context (lossless 25 fps on a bad drive) or in neither table.
4. **Internal inconsistency, §4.5 vs §5.2:** §4.5 says the raw transport of T34 M3 (COMP1 vs packed) "was not recorded"; §5.2 then states "(A)'s writer path (COMP1 → 12-bit scratch pass) is unchanged" as if COMP1 were known. The code at `6ef0ddc` selects COMP1 by stream format (`CameraController.cpp:3738-3791`), and hl:857-863 confirms COMP1 for SDR **lossless**; for SDR **uncompressed** at `4d5fdd1` the transport of C0025/C0026 remains unrecorded, as §4.5 says. §5.2's parenthesis should be labelled probable.
5. **Not an error of F5 but of the log it relies on:** hl:775 ("the numbering counts written frames, so capture gaps leave no hole") is false by code unless the speed ramp is on. F5 diagnosed this correctly (§3.1 B25); the log entry should be corrected so future readers do not treat `missing=0` as meaningless in general.

No wrong code reading found: every `path:line@6ef0ddc` cited by F5 that I opened (`4471-4480`, `9022`, `9575-9630`, `11629-11633`, `StoragePreroll.hpp:200`, `ApertarCoreApp.cpp:83-95, 9366-9377`, `1246/1364`) says what F5 says.

## 4. What F5 missed

1. **An uncounted pool of 4K lossless takes that may include 33 or 50 fps.** The 10-27 `temp:` build (Core `0bac3db`/`0296ed1`, "running on the Pi since 23:08") raised the 4K SDR lossless cap to 50 and put 25/33/50 in every fps list (handoff-T26-T30.md:104-106). The 2026-10-28 night and day shoot (38 clips, hl:879-887) ran on it, before `4d5fdd1`. These clips are on the SEAGATE disk with their DNGs; the hl:889-897 DNG-clock method (`ExifIFD:DateTimeOriginal` per full second against fps) would count them at zero rig time and could yield the first multi-minute 4K lossless datum above 25 fps. F5 §8 proposes forensics on A2 and on cinemate clips but not on these. It does not change the (A) verdict (lossless ≠ the brief's uncompressed (A), and any 50 fps clip there would be on an older build), but it is the cheapest evidence about the serial cycle at 33-50 fps under a real writer load, exactly the 22.7 vs ≤ 17.2 ms tension F5 names as undetermined.
2. **The pre-roll bound is more robust than F5 states.** F5 ties "≤ 17.2 ms" to a slack of 3-4 frames and lists the slack as open question 5. The bound 16.67 × (1 + S/114) stays under 18.1 ms for any S ≤ 10, i.e. for any plausible CFE pool. The conclusion "the lossless COMP1 path at 60 fps and 344 MB/s cycles well inside 20 ms" does not depend on the slack model. What it does depend on (and F5 says so) is that this is the COMP1 lossless path at 55 % of (A)'s writer traffic, with TDN state, WebUI state and ramp state unrecorded.
3. **A0/A1 "0 gaps" is sound despite the probable ramp-on.** F5 §3.1 B23 notes the ramp was probably on in A0 too (so `missing=0` is uninformative there as well) but does not say explicitly why A0 still counts as gap-free: `trackRecordingCadence` is independent of numbering and prints `drop[capture-gap]` at the first gap (`:9618@6ef0ddc`), and the A0 entry states "no `drop[...]` line of any kind" (hl:744). Worth one sentence, since it is the whole basis of the (B) verdict.

Nothing I found changes either verdict.

## 5. Corrected verdict and margin

- **(A) 4K SDR 12-bit uncompressed 50 fps, 10 min: OUTSIDE on the record — CONFIRMED.** Two counted takes, 12 % lost as steady single-frame capture gaps, delivered 44 fps ⇒ cycle ≈ 22.7 ms against 20.0 ms (−2.7 ms, derived), on `4d5fdd1`, 3840x2076, never-trimmed 80 %-full drive, 60 s each; sustained write shown 525 MB/s (derived from measured) against the 622 MB/s a clean take needs. No take at 3840x2160, none on a trimmed drive, none longer than 60 s, none on any later build; the completion path of `6ef0ddc` is byte-identical to `4d5fdd1` apart from pre-roll bookkeeping and an off-by-default RT knob (direct diff). The one datum on the inside, the 2 s lossless pre-roll at 60 fps (cycle ≤ 17.5 ms for any slack ≤ 6), is a different writer path at 55 % of the load. Confidence in "never observed drop-free": high (the search covered every kb file named in the assignment plus the playback and ramp folders). Confidence that it stays OUTSIDE on `6ef0ddc` by default: moderate, because the 22.7 ms figure is one build, one day, one drive state, and is in unresolved tension with the pre-roll.
- **(B) 4K Clear HDR 16-bit linear 25 fps, 10 min: INSIDE on the record — CONFIRMED,** stated as 3 of 4 ten-minute takes clean (C0101 `114bfb9` 35 % full untrimmed; A0, A1 `a40112a` trimmed, WebUI closed; A2 lossy and unexplained, probably with the ramp on), 4 of 4 three-minute (B) takes clean without the whole-fs syncfs (2 of 2 on today's default), 0 of 4 short takes clean with the WebUI open, 0 takes of any length on `d44b177`/`92d0970`/`6ef0ddc`. Cycle margin ≈ +3.8 ms at 25 fps (derived from one October 30 fps window). Confidence that it is achievable on this rig: high; that it transfers to `6ef0ddc` by default: moderate (nothing on the thread changed; `e55557d` removes one copy per frame; the pre-roll and the WebUI state are new or unrecorded).
- **(B) with Log: UNVERIFIED — CONFIRMED.** One 117-frame take with 10 gaps and the WebUI open.
- **Probability ranges in F5 §7:** keep the ordering, drop the numbers or label them "judgment": the catalogue has n = 2 for (A) and n = 4 for (B) long takes.

Corrections to carry into the synthesis: 525 MB/s not 500 for M3; "4 of 4 (2 of 2 on the default policy)" not "5 of 5" for the 3-min takes; C0016 is SDR lossless; §5.2's COMP1 for uncompressed (A) is probable, not confirmed; add the 10-28 shooting-day clips to the forensics list.
