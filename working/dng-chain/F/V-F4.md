## 3. Errors found

1. **Overstated support from A0/A1 for the per-dentry constant** (§2 #18). A0/A1 only show that the `s_lock` demand at 15,000 files fits 40 ms, i.e. c < ≈ 450–470 ns; they do not place c in the lower half of 80–250 ns. Correction: "A0/A1 exclude c ≳ 450 ns; they say nothing about where in 80–250 ns it lies." The (A) break-even stays as F4 computed it (c ≈ 80 ns → ~30,000 files; c ≈ 250 ns → ~9,000–13,000 files), but with an unconstrained c the direction of the lean is unknown.
2. **Omitted measurement: C0101** (§2 #9), a third clean 10-minute 16-bit 4K25 take (2026-10-26, 35 % full, untrimmed, 4 writers / queue 12). It strengthens (B) and is the origin of the exFAT `{4,12,0}` profile comment. It also matters for F4's trim story: a 10-minute take at 415 MB/s was clean before any trim existed, on a drive that had only ever been appended to (the stall is a post-wrap phenomenon, as F4 says).
3. **Label slip on the 733 figure** (§2 #1): "3.6 GB in 4.9 s" — the size is inferred, not logged for the direct run; and both `dd` pairs ran with libcamera streaming (preview on). State it; it does not change the number.
4. **Two separate `dd` sessions conflated as "T22"** (§2 #2): 733/556 (T22, 18:55-18:56, 3.6 GB) and 759/561-572 (00:40 the same day, 3.4 GB, clearhdr-design.md). F4 cites both correctly by file but calls 759 "a second run" of T22; q5 did the same. Harmless; n=1 per pair.
5. **48 vs 49 GB** for the 2 TB sibling's pSLC (snippet says 48). Trivial.
6. **Volume size** (§2 #21). Trivial.
7. **Incomplete direct-I/O correction** (§2 #20): F4's statement "a direct write may extend the file; what remains is the 512-B alignment" is true for a *sequential* direct writer (every write starts at `valid_size`). It omits the rule that replaced the old fallback: a direct write that starts **past** `valid_size` (a forward seek — e.g. writing the payload at an aligned offset before the header, or writing the IFD last after a gap) first zero-fills `[valid_size, pos)` through the buffered page cache. T24's IFD-first layout written front to back avoids it; any other order does not. Also `-EINVAL` on misalignment comes from `exfat_file_write_iter`, not from `exfat_direct_IO`.

No wrong code reading and no wrong arithmetic found in the budget tables (#10, #13, #14 re-derived). The exFAT model (#16, #17, #19) is right in every structural detail I could read.

## 4. What F4 missed that bears on the verdict

- **C0101** (above): (B) is 3 of 3 ten-minute takes clean on the storage side, not 2 of 2.
- **The `dd` ceilings were taken with the camera streaming**: they are already "rig-realistic" rather than bare-drive figures; the bare drive on this link may be a little faster. Neutral for (A).
- **The exFAT zero-fill rule for O_DIRECT** (lever L11): changes how the T24 patch must order its writes, not the verdict.
- **Nothing that flips either verdict.** The two unknowns that decide (A) — the drive's post-cache rate on this link and the per-dentry constant c — are the same two F4 names, and both remain unmeasured; F4's step 1 (10-min `fio --direct=1 --size=400G` with a bandwidth log) and step 2 (`variant2.py` at 50 files/s × 12,443,140 B for 600 s, or the 30,000-file `O_CREAT` loop) are the right tests.
