### 4.1 The 2026-11-01 pre-roll: 3840x2160 12-bit lossless, pinned 60 fps, 2.0 s, 115 frames, measured 60.0 fps

What it **proves** (measured + derived):
- For 115 consecutive frames at a 16.67 ms period, with the raw stream flowing to the DNG queue and four lossless-JPEG writers running at ~344 MB/s, the CFE never skipped a frame; the serial cycle's mean was ≤ ~17.2 ms (derived with ≤ 3.5 frames of CFE slack; exact slack unmeasured). This is inside the 20.0 ms period of (A) by ≥ 2.8 ms.
- The sensor, link (891 MHz), RP1 pixel-rate cap (646.7 Mpix/s on the Apertar fork) and the IPA deliver 3840x2160 12-bit at 60 fps: not a sensor/link ceiling at 50 fps. (The T34 M3 entry's guess "probably the sensor/capture side ... frame timing or CSI rate", hl:594-595, is thereby weakened: at 60 fps the same path delivered every frame for 2 s.)
- `FrameDurationLimits` must be pinned for an fps change to reach the sensor at once (a controls-only change left the sensor at ~24 fps while the log said 30): every past "fps X" claim made from the request, not the timestamps, is suspect.

What it **does not prove**:
- Nothing about 10 minutes: 2 s is before thermal steady state, before the drive's SLC/erase state matters, and only ~1 s after background writeback starts (688 MB at 344 MB/s crosses the ~350 MB dirty threshold at ~1 s).
- Nothing about the uncompressed writer load of (A): the pre-roll wrote 5.98 MB/frame through the lossless encoder (CPU-heavy, fewer bytes) where (A) writes 12.44 MB/frame through the scratch path (two full copies per frame, ~1.2 GB/s of page-cache traffic at 50 fps). The kb's one isolating measurement (buffered `dd` alone costs frames, direct `dd` does not, B3) says the writer-side DRAM/writeback traffic is exactly the term that lengthens the cycle. The pre-roll ran under about half of (A)'s writer traffic.
- Nothing about the WebUI state, the tuning (TDN), or what else ran: none recorded.
- The gap counters are muted during a pre-roll; the 60.0 figure is the only evidence, and it is sufficient for "no skip" but gives no per-frame cycle distribution.
- Why it contradicts T34 M3 (22.7 ms) is undetermined: build (`4d5fdd1` → `d44b177`; no capture-path change identified, §5), writer load (uncompressed 500 MB/s vs lossless 344), drive state (never-trimmed 80 % vs trimmed), mode (2076 vs 2160 rows, the wrong direction), duration. The cheapest discriminator is one 60 s take in each compression at 50 fps on `6ef0ddc` with `be_cycle.py` (§9).

### 4.3 The four 2026-10-31 short takes

- Build: pre-squash drop-frames (trim + Log + ram-queue code), **not** `d44b177`; nothing from `6ef0ddc`'s nine commits.
- Signature: the first gap 2-4 s in (50-100 frames); with the kb's buffer-slack model a steady deficit of S × 40 ms / N = (3-5) × 40 / (50-100) = **1.2-4 ms per frame** would be needed to exhaust the slack by then; but the subsequent rates (one gap per 12-175 frames = 0.2-3.4 ms/frame) are inconsistent across the four takes and far below a steady deficit in the longest one. Reading: a start-of-take transient (page-cache filling to the dirty threshold at ~1 s, governor, the browser's first frames, kswapd starting) plus a small steady term, not the steady overrun of M3 or A2. Derived; unverified.
- Confounders: WebUI open; reboot minutes before; an ssh query; Log in the worst take (10/117: Log adds a full-frame LUT pass on the copy worker, ~33 MB of DRAM traffic per frame — the one mechanism measured to cost capture frames — so Log is a candidate contributor, not excluded by "the earlier short takes had no Log" as hl:792 argues; that argument only shows Log is not the sole cause).

### 4.4 C0001 under the 1 s `syncfs`
12 gaps from ~113 s in a 3-min take with a once-a-second whole-filesystem `syncfs`; 0 holes with it off (C0003) and 0 with the later per-file fsync (C0007, 10-27 C0002, A0). The mechanism (a writeback burst competing with the memory-bound BE) is probable; the fix is in every build since `5b68f1f`. It is the one capture-gap case the record explains and closes. It also shows the class exists on this rig with the WebUI state unknown.

### 4.5 T34 M3: the only 4K50 data
Two 60 s takes, 355 and 358 missing, every loss a capture gap, queue 2-3/12, write failures 0, unchanged with 8 raw buffers, ~500 MB/s to a never-trimmed 80 %-full drive. Establishes (measured): storage was not the limit at the offered 500 MB/s; raw-buffer count was not the limit; the loss is upstream of Core's queue and steady. Does not establish: the cycle length (derived 22.7 ms), the raw transport (COMP1 or packed), copy-slot drops (counter not reported), the WebUI state, the tuning, preview-only delivery at 50 fps, behaviour at 3840x2160, on a trimmed drive, or for longer than 60 s. The kb's own "sensor/capture side" guess is weakened by the 60 fps pre-roll (§4.1).
