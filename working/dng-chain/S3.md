## 5. Assessment: did the drop-frames campaign make recording better?

Established (measured):
- The stall class (queue growth, `queue-full` bursts, seconds-long file times, a hung controller) is caused by drive state after ~0.9 TB without a trim, and a trim cures it (A-1, 71 synthetic runs; A0 vs the untrimmed 31 % matrix take). Core trims at mount (A-12). For an operator who records and deletes through Core's WebUI this class is gone; for one who deletes from the touch UI it is not (R4 L1: `ClipListModel::removeClip` never reaches Core).
- Log shrinks a 16-bit frame by 25 % (A-9, one frame). The ram buffer and safety stop engage (A-11, one take). The pre-roll runs at the sensor maximum (A-10, one run).

Inferred:
- "Core is clean on a trimmed drive" is one 10-min take on `a40112a`; it is consistent with six earlier long clean takes on older builds, so the **class** is well supported for linear 16.6 MB/frame at 25 fps on a healthy drive. The drop-frames build has only four short lossy takes (WebUI open), one 3-min lossless take with no drop line, one Log frame, a pre-roll and a safety-stop take (R5 N-a; hl:779-877).

Unknown:
- Whether a 10-min 4K25 16-bit take (linear and Log) is clean on `92d0970` with the WebUI closed and the pre-roll off: never run.
- The second operator case: nothing in the build touches its measured loss mechanism (capture gaps with the queue at 2-3/12; R5 C6), and no take exists on a trimmed drive, at 3840×2160, or on any build after `4d5fdd1`.
- The capture-gap class (A2, short takes, T34 M3, 30 fps) is untouched by trim, Log, ram buffer and pre-roll, and its cause is not established.

**Verdict.** Better for the stall class, and only for operators whose deletes go through Core; not shown better or worse for the two cases the operator names, which are capture-gap cases. The README's one sentence ("the dropped frames were the recording drive's state, not Apertar's buffer", README:14-15) is right for the stall class and does not cover finding 6 (short takes), which the same page lists as open.

## 9. The four assessments the brief asks for

**better_in_general.** Established: the drive-state stall (queue-full bursts after ~0.9 TB without a trim, a hung controller) is real and a trim cures it — 71 synthetic runs plus one Core take each on an untrimmed (31 % lost) and a trimmed (0 lost) drive; Core now trims at mount (927 GB, 0.47 s). Log shrinks a frame by 25 % (one frame); the ram buffer's safety stop works (one take); the pre-roll runs at 60 fps (one run). Inferred: that Core "is clean on a trimmed drive" generalises from one 10-min take on `a40112a`, consistent with six earlier long clean takes on older builds for the same class (linear 16.6 MB/frame, 25 fps, healthy drive). Unknown: no take longer than ~14 s of either operator case exists on the shipping build (`d44b177`+); its only takes lost 2-10 frames at capture with the WebUI open; nothing in the build addresses the capture-gap class, which is what both operator cases are; the trim has a hole for touch-UI deletes. Verdict: better for the stall class when deletes go through Core; unmeasured for the two named cases.

**preroll_effect.** Established: it runs (both stacks), it writes and deletes ~0.7 GB at the sensor maximum (Apertar, one run), no author ever stated a mechanism, and no before/after measurement exists anywhere. Inferred: the candidate benefits (pre-faulted copy pool, exFAT metadata in cache) act on the first frames of the next take; the candidate harms (deleted-but-untrimmed space below the 1 GiB threshold; a full fstrim queued right before a take when the pre-roll exceeds 1 GiB; a blind window of the gap counter after two fps changes) are small and specific to the port. Unknown: everything about its effect on drops; the kb's own A/B (step 0b) was never run and is one click per arm. Default-on is an untested choice; the evidence-honest default is off until E5 runs.

**clearhdr16_residual.** Established: linear uncompressed 4K25 16-bit is clean for 10 min on a healthy drive (A0, A1, C0101; 3-min C0003/C0004/C0007/C0002; 60 s C0102) on builds up to `a40112a`; the untrimmed-drive stall class is separate and cured; the lossless-JPEG encoder caps at ~18 fps at 16-bit (separate, measured). The residual is a capture-gap class: 1491 gaps in A2 (one run, with `sync_file_range` off), 2-10 gaps in four short takes with the WebUI open (10-31), 12 gaps in C0001 (10-26, sync 1 s, post-format), intermittent 7-10-frame-period gaps on 10-26 00:10. Inferred: these are CFE skips from libcamera's serial cycle exceeding 40 ms now and then; the cycle is ~36 ms while recording at 16-bit 4K (measured at 30 fps), so the margin at 25 fps is a few ms and any extra per-frame work on the completion thread (WebUI downsample) or extra DRAM traffic (buffered writes, a fourth copy for Log) can eat it. Unknown: the WebUI's actual cost; whether A2 repeats; whether the Pi's tuning has TDN; whether Log changes the gap rate; whether the drop-frames build is clean over 10 min at all. Settles with E1, E2, E3, E4c.

**sdr50_residual.** Established (one pair of 60 s takes, 2026-10-28, Core `4d5fdd1`, 3840×2076 crop, untrimmed drive at 78-82 %): 2632 of ~2987 frames, all losses capture gaps, single frames at dt 40 ms, steady through the take, `queue-full` 0, `peak_queue` 2-3/12, unchanged with 8 raw buffers; 1080p50 clean; the writer sustained 525 MB/s. Inferred: a serial IPA→BE→completion cycle of ~22.7 ms against a 20 ms period (the measured 30 fps Clear HDR signature); TDN on COMP1 input would make the BE ~29 MB/frame, which scales from the one measured BE point to 19-23 ms — marginal; link (67-70 %) and RP1 (80 %) are inside their envelopes. Unknown: the BE time at 50 fps; the raw packing that take used; whether 3840×2160 behaves like the crop; whether anything in the drop-frames build changes it (nothing targets it: Log does not apply to COMP1, the ram buffer never engages at 2-3/12, trim does not create frames the CFE skipped). Settles with E4a-c; cinemate's side has no record at all (E6).

## 10. The experiment programme (order, protocol, read-back)

Principles from the evidence review: (i) the four confounders that spoiled past readings are the WebUI state, the speed-ramp setting (D3), the drive's trim state and "what else runs on the Pi"; every take records all four; (ii) one run decides nothing — minimum three per arm or interleaved pairs; (iii) read counters from the journal, not from `takes.sh`'s `missing` alone; (iv) read-only first.

Common read-back per take (the "take record"): `journalctl -u apertar-core --since <T1>` → `recording clip:`, `recording stats:` (`enqueued`, `written`, `dropped_queue_full`, `peak_queue`, `write_failures`), `recording stats: dropped_copy_slot=` (absent means 0), `capture stats: dropped_gap_estimate=` (absent means 0), every `drop[...]` line with its timestamp, `recording stop: synced … ms`, `capture scheduling: …`, `media: trim …`, `storage preroll: …`; `ls <clip> | wc -l` and the `missing` arithmetic (valid only with the ramp off); `python3 -I core.py get_state` for `fps_speed_ramp`, `storage_preroll_auto`, `buffer_setting`, `cdng_log_applied`, `media_trim_last_unix`; `ss -tnp state established '( sport = :8080 )'` (MJPEG clients); `top -H -b -n1 | head -25`; `free -m`; NVMe temperature.
