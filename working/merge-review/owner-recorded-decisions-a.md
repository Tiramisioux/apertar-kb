2026-11-17. Collected from the KB pages Patrik's design sessions left behind, for the merge review's rework plans
(`design/*.md`), which were written without them. Read-only: no code read, nothing changed.

`tutorials/README.md`, `tutorials/progress/*.md`, `fork-log.md`.

How to read a row:
- **his words** is the KB text that records the decision. Text inside "double quotes" in that column is quoted in the
  KB as Patrik's own words; the rest is the session's record of his answer.
- "default accepted" = he took the design's default. "brief" = his project brief. "rule" = a standing rule.
- A decision that is later replaced says so, and the replacing row is named. Use the latest one.
- Rule for this review (task brief): a recorded decision stays, unless it makes a stock camera (original driver,
  720x720 screen, a 1.6.0 hardware file, no port keys) behave differently from Apertar 1.6.0; then it becomes
  something the operator turns on, not something removed.
