# Tier 2 plan (2026-11-04): 2 h supervised + up to 6 h unsupervised

Written by the cloud session after evening 3, User's own takes (`observations-2026-11-04.md`: 10 min clean at HDR16 25
uncompressed with the WebUI open; later a take with 1 lost after 3 min; lossless HDR16 drops; SDR12 ~42 fps clean) and a
forensic pass over `raw-2026-11-04.tgz` (three independent analysts; the Tier 2 candidate review and two critiques were
still running when this was written and may refine it). Labels: measured / probable / unconfirmed.

## 1. What the results say (the basis for the order)

- **TDN off removed the back-end bottleneck** (measured, evening 3): BE job 37 -> 5.9 ms, queued share 0.91 -> 0.00,
  TDN-on units lost 58 + 39 and 115 + 116, TDN-off units 1 + 0 and 2 + 0.
- **Every remaining TDN-off loss is FE Config starvation** (measured, 11 of 11; mechanism probable, strong): libcamera keeps
  only `num_cfe_config_queue` = 2 FE Config buffers in flight; a buffer goes back to the CFE only when libcamera's
  CameraManager thread handles that frame's Config dequeue, which in the qbc fork comes after the in-place 16-bit swap. When
  the swap stretches from ~8 ms to 46-81 ms, the buffer comes back after the SOF two frames on, the CFE has no job for that
  frame and skips it silently (no sequence gap, no CSI-2 count). The CFE held 3-7 raw buffers at every one of these losses.
  `be_overlap.py` section 7 replays the queue: at depth 2 it reproduces every TDN-off loss of evening 3 (u2 1, u3 1, u3r 2,
  u27 1 + 1, s28 4 in the window) and 0 in every clean take; **at depth 3 or 4 it predicts 0 everywhere**.
- **The swap stretches during kcompactd compaction bursts** (association measured, cause probable): all 11 losses fall in a
  compaction second; the bursts recur every ~32.8 s (kcompactd's proactive-compaction back-off), kcompactd ran on CPU0 at
  9 of 11 losses. User's single loss after 3 min fits this path (not measured: no log).
- Ruled out for these losses (measured): CSI-2 errors, CPU0 interrupt latency (frame starts within 0.36 ms), the 1782 Mbps
  link. The findings' "79-105 ms BE jobs" were mostly libcamera logging late (S2 + S3), not a slow back end.
- **Target 2 is met** (SDR12 33 fps 10 + 10 min, 0 lost); SDR12 has no swap, so its Config margin is large (>= 19.8 ms).
- **Lossless HDR16 at 25 fps** is limited by Core's encoder (~17.5 fps capacity, `README.md` finding 12), a separate track.
- **The preview aids (peaking, zebra, false colour, LUT) are GPU shaders on the 1280x720 YUV preview** (`architecture/
  ui.md:34, 263`), never on the raw image; denoise changes what peaking sees (a noisier preview can make peaking fire on
  noise). DNGs are the CFE buffer and do not depend on denoise.
