## 1. Every lost frame is a moment when the CFE driver had no raw buffer (measured, 8 takes)

From block 2 on, `b1-arm.sh` keeps `vb2/vb2_buf_done` only for the CFE raw node (`type == 1 && bytesused > 10000000`;
16,896,000-byte frames). Each event carries `owned_by_drv`, the raw buffers still queued to the driver after the
completion. `owned_by_drv = 0` means the driver holds no buffer for the next frame.

| Take | Arm | Lost (Core) | Completions with `owned_by_drv = 0` | Mean `owned_by_drv` (of 8) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| block 2 warm-up | sync | 9 (minutes 1/6/2) | 9 (minutes 1/6/2) | - |
| block 2 unit 1 P1 | sync | 1 | 1 | 6.53 |
| block 2 unit 1 P2 | sync | 72 | 75 | 2.56 |
| block 2 unit 2 P1 | writer | 2 | 3 | 5.36 |
| block 2 unit 2 P2 | writer | 250 | 257 | 1.91 |
| block 2 unit 3 P1 | writer | 225 | 231 | 2.08 |
| block 2 unit 3 P2 | writer | 7 | 8 | 4.85 |
| block 2 unit 4 P1 | sync | 0 | 1 | 6.40 |
| block 2 unit 4 P2 | sync | 80 | 84 | 2.24 |

The windows are the raw node's ~204 s per take (the mode switch before and the stop after add a few completions, hence
75 vs 72 etc.). Reading:

- The loss is buffer starvation at the CFE: user space (libcamera's pipeline and Core) holds all 8 raw buffers at the
  moment a frame arrives (**measured**). The CSI-2 receiver is not the path: VC0 overflows (~55 per 3 min, 1 Hz log)
  mostly fall in seconds without a loss (block 1 unit 1 P2: 1 of 12 seconds) (**probable**).
- A take is in one of two states: clean (the driver holds ~5-6.5 buffers on average, user space ~1.5-3) or losing
  (driver ~2, user space ~6) (**measured**, 8 takes). `writer` lowers the driver's share by ~1 buffer in both states
  (**measured**, n = 1-2 per cell).
- It happens without reclaim: the sync takes had `pgsteal_kswapd` 0-58 k against 3.3-4.1 M under writer, and both
  warm-ups (sync, `pgsteal_kswapd` 0) lost frames (17 and 9) (**measured**).

## 2. Which take of a unit loses is not fixed (measured; corrects an interim reading)

Per unit, one of the two takes usually falls into the losing state and the other stays near clean:
writer P1/P2 = 357/50, 280/40, 2/250, 225/7; sync = 1/47, 0/108, 1/72, 0/80. "The second take of a Core process holds the
buffers longer" (said during the evening after block 2 unit 1-2) is **refuted** by block 2 unit 3 (225 then 7).
What puts a take into the losing state is **unconfirmed**.

## 3. G1 does not separate the states cleanly (measured)

CameraManager IPC 1.49-1.53 in clean sync takes, 1.40-1.46 in writer takes whether losing or not (block 2 unit 3 P2:
1.40 with 7 lost). Lower IPC goes with writer more than with losing (**probable**, n small).

## 4. Method notes from the evening

- `csi2_errors` (debugfs) is cleared on every read: one reader only during a take (the 1 Hz logger).
- A camera mode change replaces Core's CameraManager threads: G1 must pick its thread at `record_start`.
- Unfiltered `vb2_buf_done` (~45 events per frame, ISP nodes) overran CPU0's 8 MiB buffer within one unit; block 1's
  traces lost P1 and the unit-4 window. Block 1's buffer-state reading is therefore partial.
- `v4l2/vb2_v4l2_buf_done` (the sequence numbers M3 wanted) never fires on this stack: M3 needs another source.
- The NVMe cools from ~57-62 C to 40 C in 15-20 min: the cool-down sets the pace (~30 min per unit).

## 5. Open, for after B1

- Whether the 8-buffer margin itself is the cheapest lever (`APERTAR_RAW_CAMERA_BUFFERS`, cf. the 12-buffer
  `zzz-ab-buffers.conf` once installed): a test question, not a recommendation.
